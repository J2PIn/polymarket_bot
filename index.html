FAQ

Does this bot make money?

No promise is made. The bot is an execution and research tool. Whether a strategy is profitable depends on your market selection, configuration, risk management, fees, liquidity, timing, and live conditions.

Is this financial advice?

No. This product is not financial advice, not investment advice, and not a recommendation to trade any market.

Can I run it in dry-run mode?

Yes. The intended workflow is to run dry-run first, inspect logs, adjust configuration, and only then consider live mode.

Why is Starter $849?

Because the value is not the file count. The value is the accumulated execution work: live market discovery, rollover handling, orderbook behavior, wallet approvals, settlement checks, logs, run summaries, and risk gates. If you are serious about testing your own strategy with real capital, the price should be small relative to the time and mistakes it can save.

Which markets can it support?

The engine is designed around Polymarket-style binary/event markets. Example directions include crypto, finance, weather, and sports, depending on market availability and configuration.

Do I need to be technical?

Yes, at least for the Starter plan. Operator is better if you want help with setup, configuration, and first-run review.
